Tranquilo.art is seeking volunteers

Tranquilo.art is looking for art lovers who are interested in volunteering their time to make public domain art free for all to enjoy.

Prefer to give money instead of time? You can also support us directly.

Art Storyteller — Content Curation

Volunteer, part time

Do you enjoy helping other people enjoy art? Do you find art history fascinating? Tranquilo.art is a passion project that aims to give everyone a calm scrolling experience where they can enjoy art and become immersed in the story of the art. It's a free product, and we're seeking volunteers to help curate our 9k+ item catalogue of public domain art.

What you'll do:

  • Write narrative prose about art works in the Tranquilo catalog
  • Curate collections of art around narrative stories
  • Fact-check the history or provenance of works
  • Recommend adjustments to the Tranquilo catalog and help with procurement strategy for future items

Preferred background & time expectations:

  • Art history or humanities studies
  • Solid writing skills with a good command of English grammar and prose
  • 3-5 hours per week is preferred

What we can offer:

  • Flexible timing; work when it suits you best
  • See your content live — use your work here to help grow your portfolio and resume
  • We are willing to collaborate with university programs for school credit or other arrangements that could benefit students

Interested? Reach out to Mel directly: hello@tranquilo.art
