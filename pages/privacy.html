Privacy Policy

Short version: Tranquilo doesn't collect personal information about you. There are no accounts, no cookies, and nothing that follows you from one visit to the next. This page explains that in a bit more detail.

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Tranquilo is operated by Cora Studios ME, a microempresa registered in Belo Horizonte, Brazil. Brazil's data protection law (LGPD) applies to personal data — information that can identify a person. We don't collect any on this site, so there isn't any to protect, sell, or lose. Keeping it that way is the point.

Our Terms of Service cover everything else.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts. You can't sign up, so we never have a name, email, or password from you.
  • No cookies. We don't set tracking cookies, and there are no advertising or social-media trackers on the site.
  • No identifiers. We don't assign you a user ID or a session ID, and we don't track you across visits or across devices.
  • No profiles. We don't build a picture of who you are, and we have nothing to sell to anyone.

What we do measure

We keep count of how the site is being used, so we know whether it's working and can describe it honestly to the institutions and supporters we work with.

  • Visits & Network Security — roughly how many people come to the site, which country they're in, and what browser and kind of device they're using. This is handled by Cloudflare, our hosting provider. Additionally, Vercel provides infrastructure security and bot protection. When you request a page, network firewalls temporarily inspect network metadata (like your IP address) in real time to protect the site from DDoS attacks, malicious traffic, and automated abuse. These edge security logs are strictly used for infrastructure defence, kept separate from any site analytics, and deleted automatically.
  • Feature use — counts of things like which category labels get clicked, whether music is turned on, how often share links are used, and how often art stories are opened. These are stored in our database (Neon).
  • Search terms — the words typed into the search box, so we can see what people are looking for and go and find more of it. These are stored on their own, with nothing attached: we can see that someone searched for "Vermeer", and not who, or what else they did.

All of it is anonymous by design. In our own analytics and database, there's no user ID, no session ID, no stored IP address, and no way to connect one action to another or to link anything back to you. We can see that a share link was clicked 400 times. We can't see who clicked it, or that the same visitor did anything twice.

We built it this way deliberately, not as an afterthought. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

What stays in your browser

Your collection is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on the device you're using — we don't set cookies, and your collection is never sent to us. Export simply reads that local copy and builds the CSV file on your device.

A few small settings live in the same place: whether music is on, whether you've dismissed a prompt, and your recent searches for the length of the visit. None of it leaves your device.

We can't see any of it. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and we have no copy to restore.

If you contact us

If you email us, send feedback, or submit a collection, we'll use what you give us to respond to you and handle your request. That's it — we don't add you to anything or share it with anyone. Email is handled through Resend, our email provider.

If you sign up for occasional updates, we use your email address to send them and nothing else — also through Resend. Every message has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes your address.

Donations

If you choose to support Tranquilo financially, payment is handled entirely by Stripe. They collect your payment details under their own privacy policy, and keep them on file if you set up a recurring donation, until you cancel it. We never see or store your card or bank information.

If we add other ways to donate later, we'll update this page.

Why we're allowed to hold the little we hold

Brazilian data protection law (LGPD) asks us to say what our basis is for each thing:

  • Newsletter — your consent. You gave it by subscribing, and you can take it back at any time.
  • Messages you send us — your own request. You wrote to us, so we use what you sent to write back, and for nothing else.
  • Donations — Stripe processes what's needed to complete the payment you asked for, under its own terms.
  • Network security & abuse prevention — legitimate interest. Infrastructure providers (Vercel and Cloudflare) process temporary network logs (like IP addresses) solely to protect the platform against automated bots, spam, and cyberattacks.

That's the whole list, because that's all the personal information there is.

Your rights

For that small amount of information, LGPD gives you the right to ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold anything about you, and show you what
  • correct anything wrong or incomplete
  • delete it
  • give you a copy you can take elsewhere
  • tell you who we've shared it with
  • withdraw your consent, where consent is what we relied on

Email hello@tranquilo.art and we'll do it. No form, no charge, no reason required. We'll reply as quickly as we can, and within the deadlines LGPD sets.

If you've only ever browsed the site, these rights have nothing to attach to — we don't hold anything about you to show, correct, or delete.

You can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority, the ANPD, if you think we've handled something badly.

Children

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to disclose.

Where the data lives

Our hosting and analytics providers (Cloudflare, Vercel, Neon) operate servers around the world, so anonymous usage counts may be processed outside Brazil. Because none of it identifies a person, there's no personal data crossing a border there.

Where personal information does exist — your email address with Resend if you write to us or subscribe, and your payment details with Stripe if you donate — it is handled by companies based in the United States, so that information is processed outside Brazil. Each is bound by its own contractual and legal safeguards, and in Stripe's case we never hold the information ourselves at all.

How long we keep things

Visit statistics and security logs sit with Cloudflare and Vercel and are kept only as long as required for infrastructure protection and security monitoring — see Cloudflare's and Vercel's respective privacy policies for details.

The anonymous feature-use counts and search terms in our own database are kept as long as they're useful for understanding how Tranquilo is doing. Messages you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with them. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. We'd rather over-explain a change than let this page quietly go out of date.

Getting in touch

Questions about privacy, requests about your data, or anything on this page: hello@tranquilo.art

This is our official channel for data protection matters. As a microempresa, we aren't required to appoint a data protection officer, and this address stands in place of one — a real person reads it.
